Urban test environment

Loading city geometry…

Open lawns, parks, wider roads and scattered buildings are included. Trees and decorative street furniture are visual-only; structural buildings are collision solids.

Urban canyon buildings and roads are procedurally generated with a fixed seed. Buildings have matching physical MuJoCo colliders. This is a city-scale research scene, not a georeferenced OpenStreetMap digital twin.

Environment

Inspection complex: industrial blocks, cargo container, antenna tower, pylon, and overhead beam with MuJoCo collision geometry. Mission planner first climbs to a 7 m transit corridor above the tallest structure, then follows elevated waypoints and returns to base.
