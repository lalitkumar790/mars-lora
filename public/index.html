When a drone is connected, is its information still on time?

A reproducible exploration of communication freshness, alert deadlines, and mission overhead for low-bandwidth LoRa links in autonomous UAV inspection.

Raw LoRa • kinematic simulator • deterministic seeded trials
Model scope: This is research simulation, not a field-tested radio or a physically validated autonomous drone. Generated graphics, when present, represent executed synthetic-model trials only.

System and research methodology

01 / UAV inspection route

Rectangular 900 m route; nominal 2, 5, 9 m/s. One ground station; geometric path loss and synthetic obstruction.

02 / Link simulator

LoRa SF7–12, airtime, correlated fading, synthetic 10% external occupancy, constrained delivery queue and message deadlines.

03 / Four policies

Fixed SF9, reactive SF, priority-aware traffic, and conservative MARS slowdown. Compare delivery, freshness, alert deadline reliability, and mission time.

Research prototype • All raw experiment generators and manuscript source are available on GitHub. The static Vercel site presents results; it does not execute MuJoCo or ROS 2.
